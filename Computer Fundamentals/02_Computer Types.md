# Task 2: Sophia's Summer of Hidden Computers – Month 1

During her first month at Nova Labs, Sophia learned that different computers are designed for different tasks.

The room introduces four common computer types.

| Computer Type | Screen & Keyboard | Main Purpose |
|---------------|-------------------|--------------|
| Laptop | Yes | Portable everyday computing |
| Desktop | Yes | Sustained performance at a fixed location |
| Workstation | Yes | Precision and reliability for professional tasks |
| Server | No | Providing services to many users over a network |

### Laptop

Sophia first used a laptop, which was ideal for everyday work such as browsing the internet and writing documents. Since laptops are designed to be portable, they may slow down during long or demanding tasks.

### Desktop

She then used a desktop computer. Unlike a laptop, it stays in one place, uses wall power, and has better cooling, allowing it to provide more consistent performance.

### Workstation

Next, Sophia learned about workstations. Although they look similar to desktops, they are built with specialized components for professional work like simulations and 3D models.

### Server

Finally, Sophia visited a room filled with servers. Servers usually don't have a dedicated screen or keyboard because they provide services to multiple users over a network.

# Task 3: Sophia's Summer of Hidden Computers – Month 2

During her second month, Sophia started noticing computers hidden inside everyday devices.

The room introduces four additional computer types.

| Type | Description | Examples |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Smartphone | Pocket-sized computer optimized for battery life and connectivity | iPhone, Android phone |
| Tablet | Touch-first computer with a larger screen | iPad, Drawing tablet |
| IoT Device | Network-connected device with a single purpose | Smart thermostat, Smart doorbell, Fitness tracker |
| Embedded Computer | Computer built into another device | Coffee machine, Automatic door sensor |

### IoT vs Embedded Computers

Although both are designed for specific tasks, there is one important difference.

- **IoT devices** connect to a network to send or receive data.
- **Embedded computers** perform their task inside a device and may never connect to a network.

### Questions

**What is the currently most popular pocket-sized computer?**

```text
Smartphone
```

**What kind of computer would you expect to find in a coffee machine?**

```text
Embedded Computer
```
